Emilyguitar, Karoryfer Samples, played and mapped by D. Smolken
https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.emilyguitar

Epiphone Emily the Strange guitar with d'Addario Chromes flatwound strings,
recorded direct using both pickups with all knobs on ten.

Published under the Creative Commons CC0 public domain dedication:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Files are named <MIDI note>_<layer>_<take>.opus for notes, where layers 1, 2
and 3 are the original mp, mf and f velocity layers, and
<MIDI note>_release_<take>.opus for the string muting noises played on note
release. Notes were cut to 3 seconds and everything was encoded to mono Opus
at 128 kbps.
